The hold is the gate

A leaked code buys an account that can't do anything. There's no value to steal before a human looks.

Claims make review fast

Manager and office aren't verified at entry — they're what Nico or Justin recognizes or doesn't in about a minute.

Weak signals do the sorting

Blank manager, odd hours, repeat IPs. Nothing blocks on its own, but it puts the right rows at the top.

Denial is quiet

Nothing is revoked or announced — the account simply never leaves restricted state and expires.

This is a bridge

When SSO or a roster lands, the gate swaps out and the queue empties. The restricted state stays useful.
